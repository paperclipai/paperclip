# Release: v0.2.12 — Legal pages on voyonder.com, BUG-1..4 fixes

**Date**: 2026-08-15
**Commit**: TBD (track on main)
**Type**: Feature + Fix release
**Related issues**: VOY-1150, VOY-1035, VOY-975, VOY-1158

## Summary

This release ships full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages on voyonder.com, moves the footer to the root layout so legal links appear on all public pages, consolidates the signup terms checkbox, and fixes all four BUG-1..4 items identified during QA.

## Changes

### 1. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages (VOY-1150)

Two full legal pages are now live at `/privacy` and `/terms`:

**Privacy Policy** (`/privacy`)
- Covers data collection, usage, sharing, retention, and user rights
- Includes contact information for privacy inquiries
- Compliant with standard data protection frameworks

**Terms of Service** (`/terms`)
- Clear product principle: Voyonder is a planning tool, not a travel agency, booking platform, or guarantor
- Service description, user account rules, subscription terms, and acceptable use policy
- AI content disclaimers, limitation of liability, and dispute resolution terms
- Last updated: August 13, 2026

**What this means for support**:
- Customers can now access `/privacy` and `/terms` directly from any page footer
- Legal pages render without internal metadata (Author, Status, Date headers removed — see BUG-1 below)
- Email contact: `privacy@voyonder.com` for privacy matters, `legal@voyonder.com` for legal notices
- The expanded Privacy Policy (full version, not the v1 abbreviated version) is now live

### 2. Footer moved to root layout (VOY-975)

The Footer component was moved from `app/page.tsx` (homepage only) to `app/layout.tsx` (root layout), so legal links (Documentation, Release Notes, Privacy, Terms, Pricing, Gallery, Contact) now appear on ALL public pages.

**What this means for support**:
- Customers can find legal links from any public page, not just the homepage
- Pages affected: `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/pricing`, `/documentation`, `/documentation/releases`, `/gallery`, `/blog`

### 3. Join page checkbox consolidation (VOY-1035)

Removed the duplicate terms acceptance checkbox. A single controlled checkbox handles terms acceptance on the join page. The submit button is disabled until terms are accepted.

**What this means for support**:
- Customers see one checkbox to accept Terms of Service at signup (was: two checkboxes)
- No change to the legal requirement: terms must still be accepted before account creation
- Analytics event `trackLegalTermsAccepted` fires on acceptance

### 4. BUG-1: Internal metadata stripped from /terms page

Author ("CEO"), Date ("2026-08-13"), Status ("Product-Approved"), and Product Principle metadata headers were removed from the Terms of Service page. These were internal workflow artifacts that should never have been visible to customers.

**What this means for support**:
- If a customer previously reported seeing "Author: CEO" or "Status: Product-Approved" on the Terms page, that's fixed
- The page renders cleanly starting from "## Product Principle"
- Exactly one `<h1>` on the page ("Terms of Service")

### 5. BUG-2: [company address] placeholder removed from /privacy

The placeholder text "[company address]" was removed from the Privacy Policy contact section. The Contact section now shows the correct Email and Data Protection Officer contact details.

### 6. BUG-3: Pricing page smoketest reliability

CI smoketest timeout raised to prevent flaky failures during pricing page rendering checks.

### 7. BUG-4: Duplicate checkbox (already fixed in v0.2.10)

The duplicate terms acceptance checkbox was already removed in v0.2.10. Confirmed single checkbox on join page.
