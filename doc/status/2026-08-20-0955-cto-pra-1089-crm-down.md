- **crm.praesyn.com** is served by Traefik (vps-2, host networking) →
  `http://127.0.0.1:3001` → Twenty CRM container `crm` (NestJS, port 3000).
- Traefik health-check log shows the `crm` backend was failing from at least
  **09:26 UTC** with `connection refused` → `EOF` → `connection reset by peer`
  every 30s — the CRM container was down or crash-looping.
- The `crm` container was recreated at **09:47:37 UTC** (docker events show
  network re-attaches at 09:35, 09:44, 09:47 — multiple recreate attempts).
- Nest finished booting at **09:50:59 UTC** (`Nest application successfully started`),
  Traefik health check recovered, and the site came back at ~09:51 UTC.
- Container state now: `running`, `OOMKilled=false`, `RestartCount=0`.
- **Root trigger:** residual instability from the Aug 19–20 vps-2 cAdvisor OOM
  incident (112+ zombie wget processes, PRA-1047/PRA-1061) — the CRM container
  had been killed/recreated during the memory-pressure cascade and took ~3 min
  to fully boot Nest. No new OOM or crash since recovery.
