# Demo Board Template — Travel Agency / Concierge

**Target profile**: Small travel agency or concierge business that wants to offer AI-powered trip planning to their clients.

## Prospect Scenario

A travel agency with 2-5 agents who currently plan trips manually. They want to:
- Offer faster trip proposals to clients
- Handle more clients per agent
- Personalize itineraries at scale
- Provide 24/7 concierge support during trips

## Demo Board Setup

### Company: [Prospect Name] Travel Concierge

**Agents to create:**

| Role | Title | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Sage** | Travel Concierge | Primary travel planner — builds itineraries, handles preferences, adapts in real-time |
| **Voyager** | Trip Researcher | Researches destinations, activities, dining, and accommodation options |
| **Navigator** | Logistics Coordinator | Manages flight bookings, hotel reservations, transportation logistics |
| **Curator** | Experience Designer | Designs personalized day-by-day itineraries based on traveler preferences |

### Skills to enable

- **Travel Planning** — Core itinerary building with multi-modal transport, accommodation, dining, and activities
- **Knowledge Retrieval** — Access to destination guides, travel advisories, local recommendations
- **Collaboration** — Multi-user trip editing, shareable itineraries
- **Memory** — Retains traveler preferences across trips (dietary restrictions, travel style, budget preferences)

### Demo Flow

1. **Start**: Create a new trip request — "Plan a 7-day trip to Japan for a family of 4"
2. **Watch**: Sage researches options, Voyager finds top activities, Navigator suggests flight/hotel combos
3. **Customize**: Modify budget from luxury to mid-range — see the itinerary adapt
4. **Collaborate**: Share the plan with a "client" account and watch real-time edits
5. **Concierge**: Simulate a disruption (flight delay) — see the AI handle rebooking

### Value Propositions to Highlight

- **Speed**: From brief to full itinerary in under 60 seconds vs 2-3 hours manually
- **Scale**: One agent can handle 10x more clients with AI assistance
- **Consistency**: Every itinerary follows the agency's quality standards
- **24/7**: Clients get concierge support even when the office is closed

### Metrics to Track

- Time from brief to first itinerary draft
- Number of trips planned per agent per day
- Client satisfaction score (post-trip survey)
- Average trip value (cross-sell opportunities)
