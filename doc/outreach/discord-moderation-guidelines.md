# Voyonder Discord — Moderation Guidelines

*Ready to post in #rules channel. Updated Aug 2026.*

## Voyonder Community Guidelines

### Our Ethos

This is a community for **builders, travelers, and early adopters**. We're here to shape the future of AI-powered trip planning together. Every member — whether you're a beta tester, a contributor, or just curious — deserves a constructive, respectful experience.

### Rules

#### 1. Be Constructive
Critique **ideas**, not people. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments are not. This is a community for builders — challenge the product, not each other.

#### 2. No Self-Promotion or Spam
Share your Voyonder-built trips and use cases in #showcase. Unsolicited links to unrelated products, services, or crypto/NFT projects will be removed. If you're unsure whether something belongs, ask a Moderator first.

#### 3. No Confidential or NDA-Breaching Content
Do not share access keys, API credentials, internal documentation, or any material covered by non-disclosure agreements. We want an open community, but some things stay in private channels.

#### 4. Tag Responsibly
Use @mentions sparingly. @everyone and @here are reserved for Moderators and Admins for urgent announcements. Direct questions to the appropriate channel rather than pinging individuals.

#### 5. Report Issues
See something that violates these guidelines? Tag a **@Moderator** or use `/report` — don't engage or argue in the thread. Moderators will handle it.

#### 6. Stay On-Topic Per Channel
Each channel has a purpose (see the channel guide in #welcome). Keep discussions in the right channel. Off-topic posts may be moved or removed by Moderators.

#### 7. No Impersonation
Do not impersonate Voyonder staff, Moderators, or other community members.

### Moderation Actions

| Action | Trigger | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Warning** | First minor infraction | DM from Moderator with rule reference |
| **Message removal** | Off-topic post, minor rule violation | Removed with optional note |
| **Temporary mute** (1h–24h) | Repeated minor violations | Applies to the offending channel |
| **Kick** | Egregious violation (harassment, spam) | Can rejoin with warning |
| **Ban** | Severe or repeated violations after kick | Permanent — no appeal within 30 days |

### Moderator Code of Conduct

Moderators agree to:
- Enforce rules consistently — no favoritism
- Document moderation actions (screenshot + reason)
- Escalate ambiguous cases to @Admin
- Never use mod powers for personal disputes
- Maintain confidentiality of mod-channel discussions

### Appeals

If you believe a moderation action was taken in error:
1. DM a different **@Moderator** or @Admin
2. Explain what happened and why you think it's wrong
3. A second moderator will review within 48 hours

### Updates

These guidelines may be updated as the community grows. Major changes will be announced in #announcements.
