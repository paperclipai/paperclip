# Voyonder Beta Demo Script

## Goal
Demonstrate Voyonder's core value proposition in under 10 minutes: AI-powered travel planning with Sage, the personal travel concierge.

## Setup
- Open https://voyonder.com in a browser
- Ensure you're logged in with a demo account
- Have Sage chat open and ready

---

## Script (10 minutes)

### 1. Introduction (30 seconds)

> "Hey {{prospect_name}}, thanks for jumping on. Voyonder is an AI-powered travel concierge — think of Sage as a personal travel assistant that handles everything from planning to real-time adjustments. Let me show you what I mean."

### 2. Start a Trip — The "Hello World" (2 minutes)

> "Let's start a new trip. I'll tell Sage what I need."

**Type into Sage chat:**
```
"Plan a 5-day trip to Tokyo for next month. I'm interested in food, culture, and avoiding tourist traps. Budget is mid-range."
```

> "Watch Sage build an itinerary. It considers your preferences, budget, and timing, and generates a structured plan with flights, hotels, activities, and dining — all personalized."

**Highlight:**
- Sage's response with the structured itinerary
- The reasoning behind each recommendation
- Multiple options presented (not just one)

### 3. Refine & Customize (2 minutes)

> "Now let's customize. Maybe I don't like one of the suggestions."

**Type:**
```
"Actually, I'm not into sushi. Swap the sushi tour for a cooking class instead."
```

> "Sage immediately revises the plan — no friction, no starting over. This is where AI really shines: adaptive, conversational planning."

**Highlight:**
- The real-time revision
- How Sage remembers preferences throughout the conversation

### 4. Collaboration (1.5 minutes)

> "Traveling with others? Voyonder makes group planning easy."

**Show:**
- The Share button on the itinerary
- How collaborators can be invited
- Real-time editing by multiple people

> "Everyone sees the same plan, can suggest changes, and Sage incorporates everyone's preferences."

### 5. Concierge Support (2 minutes)

> "What about mid-trip changes? This is where Sage really earns its name."

**Type:**
```
"My flight got delayed 3 hours. What should I do?"
```

> "Sage adjusts the itinerary — reschedules activities, recommends nearby cafes for the wait, and rearranges the rest of the day. No frantic googling, no calling the hotel."

**Highlight:**
- The real-time responsiveness
- Practical, actionable suggestions
- The "concierge" feel of having a dedicated assistant

### 6. Knowledge & Personalization (1 minute)

> "Over time, Sage learns your preferences and builds a knowledge base about you."

**Show:**
- Saved preferences (dietary restrictions, travel style, favorites)
- Previous trip history
- How they inform future recommendations

### 7. Call to Action (1 minute)

> "That's the core experience. Here's what I'd love from you:
> 1. **Use Voyonder to plan your next trip** — real trip, real feedback
> 2. **Share anything confusing or missing** — we're building this for you
> 3. **Tell me what would make it indispensable**
>
> Questions so far?"

### 8. Wrap-up

> "Great. I'll send you a welcome email with a quickstart guide and an invite to our community Discord. Your feedback feeds directly into our roadmap — you're literally shaping the product."

**Send:** Welcome email + invite to Discord

## Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I usually book everything myself" | "That's exactly who we built this for — Sage handles the research and logistics so you can focus on the experience." |
| "I use a travel agent" | "Think of Sage as your own AI travel agent — available 24/7, remembers everything, and costs a fraction of traditional agents." |
| "Is this just ChatGPT with travel prompts?" | "Sage is purpose-built for travel — it understands itineraries, knows about flights/hotels/activities booking systems, remembers your preferences across trips, and collaborates in real-time with other travelers." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. Let me send you a quick video of Sage in action, and feel free to join our community Discord to see what others are building. No pressure." |
| "Is it safe with my payment info?" | "We use Stripe for payments. Your trip details and preferences are private to you. We never share personal data." |

## Technical Requirements

- Stable internet connection
- Modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Screen sharing capability for the call
- A prepared demo account with some history (preferences, past trip) for a richer demo
