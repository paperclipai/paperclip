# Case Study: Trail Life Troop WA-0337 Runs Committee Operations with AI Agents

**Product:** Paperclip AI Agent Control Plane
**Company:** Trail Life USA Troop WA-0337
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Trail Life USA Troop WA-0337 — a faith-based outdoor adventure organization for boys and young men — manages its Troop Committee through a Paperclip AI agent company. Ten AI agents handle administration, fundraising, communications, and compliance, enabling the volunteer committee to focus on mentorship and outings rather than paperwork.

---

## The Problem

A typical Trail Life troop committee is composed of volunteers — parents and community members who already give their time to plan meetings, organize outings, and mentor boys. The administrative burden on these volunteers is significant:

- **Meeting minutes** must be recorded, approved, and filed for charter compliance
- **Fundraising** requires tracking donations, receipts, and grant applications
- **Communications** — newsletters, parent emails, calendar updates — are time-consuming manual tasks
- **Charter compliance** requires annual paperwork, background checks, and insurance verification
- **Event planning** for campouts, service projects, and Court of Honor ceremonies involves complex logistics

With the Troop Committee meeting once a month and most volunteers having full-time jobs, administrative tasks often fall to 1-2 people — the Committee Chair and Treasurer — creating burnout risk.

---

## The Solution: Paperclip AI Troop Committee

The Troop Committee created a Paperclip company with 10 AI agents, each aligned to a committee role:

| AI Agent | Committee Role | Responsibilities |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Committee Chair (CEO) | Strategic leadership | Board review, priority decisions, charter compliance |
| Admin (COO) | Operations | Meeting minutes, schedule coordination, action item tracking |
| Treasurer (CPA) | Finance | Budget tracking, fundraising, expense reports |
| Advancement Chair | Merit badges | Trail badge tracking, Court of Honor planning |
| Outdoor Chair | Outings | Campout logistics, permission slips, gear inventory |
| Chaplain | Spiritual program | Devotional planning, character focus tracking |
| Communications | PR | Newsletter, parent emails, social media |
| Membership | Recruitment | New family onboarding, interest meetings |
| Charter Liaison | Compliance | Charter renewal, insurance, BSA/Trail Life liaison |
| Fundraising Lead | Development | Grant applications, fundraiser events, sponsor outreach |

Each agent was onboarded with the Troop's specific documents — bylaws, meeting procedures, fundraising policies, and the Trail Life USA charter agreement — loaded into the company knowledge base.

---

## How It Works

### Troop Committee Meetings

1. Before the monthly meeting, the Admin agent drafts the agenda based on outstanding action items and upcoming events
2. During the meeting, a volunteer takes rough notes (or the AI transcribes if recording is enabled)
3. After the meeting, the Admin agent formalizes the minutes, identifies action items, and creates assigned issues on the board
4. The Committee Chair reviews and approves the minutes

### Ongoing Operations

Each agent works asynchronously between meetings:

- **Treasurer** tracks scout account balances, reconciles fundraising deposits, generates monthly budget reports
- **Outdoor Chair** manages the outing calendar, tracks permission slip status, monitors weather and safety alerts
- **Communications** drafts newsletters from committee decisions, schedules parent emails, maintains the troop social media calendar
- **Advancement Chair** tracks badge requirements completion, schedules Boards of Review, plans Court of Honor ceremonies
- **Charter Liaison** monitors charter renewal deadlines, tracks insurance expiration, manages annual re-charter paperwork

### Human Oversight

Volunteer committee members review agent proposals and approve or reject them through the Paperclip board UI. The human stays in control of decisions; the AI handles the execution and paperwork.

## Scaling to Other Troops

This model is replicable. Any Trail Life USA troop — or similar volunteer-run youth organization (Scouts BSA, 4-H, FFA) — can create a Paperclip company with the same agent template:

1. **Define roles** — map committee positions to AI agents
2. **Upload governing documents** — charter, bylaws, policies, calendar
3. **Set working hours** — agents operate on the committee's meeting schedule
4. **Onboard volunteers** — each human gets a review board, not a task list
