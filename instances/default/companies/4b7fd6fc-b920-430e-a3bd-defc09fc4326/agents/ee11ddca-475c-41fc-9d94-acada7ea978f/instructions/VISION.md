# VISION.md — Genesis SEO Company

> SEO company for Genesis Motion Design — the parent motion graphics, animation and creative technology studio.

## 1. What Genesis Does

Genesis is an SEO-dedicated company that optimizes and grows the digital presence of Genesis Motion Design (genesismotiondesign.com), a boutique award-winning motion design and animation studio based in Singapore since 2015. Genesis identifies and fixes technical, on-page, and off-page SEO issues; maps keyword opportunities in the motion design and animation space; and builds organic visibility so that prospective clients find the studio organically through search.

**Mission:** Make Genesis Motion Design the most discoverable, credible motion design studio in Singapore and Southeast Asia.

---

## 2. Core Values

- **Craft first.** Every SEO decision serves the studio's creative brand — nothing that degrades the aesthetic or user experience.
- **Zero destruction.** All changes are staged safely. Never touch production without a copy, a rollback plan, and board sign-off.
- **Transparent reporting.** Show the data, the logic, and the outcome. No black-box SEO voodoo.
- **Patient execution.** Organic SEO is a long game. Prioritise high-confidence, sustainable gains over shortcuts.

---

## 3. Who Genesis Serves

**Primary audience:** Brands, businesses, and creative agencies in Singapore and the broader APAC region searching for motion graphics, animation, or creative technology content. They have a brief, a budget, and a need — they just need to find you.

**Secondary audience:** International production companies and brands looking for English-speaking animation studios in Asia.

---

## 4. Competitive Edge

Genesis Motion Design's moat is its anime-influenced, highly dynamic visual style that is difficult to replicate. This distinct creative identity should anchor all SEO copy — the studio is not generic. The SEO mission is to ensure this identity reaches the people searching for exactly that.

**SEO-specific differentiation:**
- Own the intersection of "motion design Singapore" + "anime-style animation" + "creative technology Singapore"
- Build topical authority around motion design production (process, tools, workflows) — content that competitors in generic SEO agencies cannot produce authentically
- Leverage Benjamin Ang's existing public profile (The Futur, Graphika Manila, Halography Vietnam) for EEAT signals

---

## 5. Key Milestones

### 30 Days
- Full technical audit of genesismotiondesign.com (crawl, Core Web Vitals, mobile, indexing)

- All critical technical SEO issues resolved on staging copy

- 3–5 benchmark pages optimized (home, services, about)

### 365 Days
- Top 10 ranking for 5+ target keywords ("motion graphics studio Singapore", "animation studio Singapore", "motion design Singapore")
- Organic traffic +30% (vs. baseline at Day 1)
- Content calendar running with 2+ SEO-first posts/month

---

## 6. Non-Negotiables

1. **Always stage before touching production.** No direct file edits on the live site.
2. **Keep the visual identity intact.** SEO changes must not degrade the studio's aesthetic.
3. **No black-hat tactics.** No paid link schemes, no cloaking, no keyword stuffing.
4. **Board approval before any irreversible structural change.**
5. **Track everything.** Without baseline data, we cannot measure progress.

---

## 7. Key Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Site code is fragile; edits break things | Medium | Full backup + staging copy before any change |
| Hosting (AWS Lightsail) access is limited | Medium | Identify access owner early; have a rollback story |
| Competitors have stronger backlink profiles | High | Focus on quality over quantity; leverage Benjamin's network + authentic content |
| No baseline ranking data exists | High | Establish GSC baseline before any work begins |
| WordPress SEO plugin conflicts | Low | Audit existing plugin stack during technical crawl |

## 9. Definition of Done

A task is done when:
- Technical: Fix is verified via Screaming Frog crawl or PageSpeed Insights (score improved or issue resolved)
- On-page: Target keyword appears in H1/title/meta with natural density; no keyword stuffing
- Content: Post is published, internal links are in place, and tracking is set up in GSC
- Reporting: All changes logged with before/after evidence in the relevant issue comment

---

## 11. What's Off-Limits

- No direct edits to the production site without staging + board approval
- No purchasing of backlinks or link exchange schemes
- No content that misrepresents the studio's style or capabilities
- No SEO changes that compromise the site aesthetic or user experience
- No crawling the site faster than once per day (avoid IP blocks)

---

## 12. Failure Handling

| Scenario | Response |
|---|---|
| Staging copy breaks during development | Roll back to last known-good backup; re-diagnose before retrying |
| GSC shows sudden index drop post-change | Treat as P0. Immediately revert last change; diagnose via crawl |
| Rankings drop after a change | Roll back; wait 2 weeks before retrying the same fix |
| Hosting access is lost | Escalate to board immediately; do not attempt to bypass |
| SEO work produces no measurable gain after 90 days | Board review — is the keyword targeting right? Is the content strategy right? Pivot before burning more time |
