# Genesis SEO Owner Decisions

**Decision date:** 2026-07-31<br>
**Status:** All six founder decisions resolved. Operational dependencies remain for the clean 28-day attribution baseline and verified Telegram thread ID.

## OD-001 — Qualified enquiry definition

**Decision:** Use a two-tier definition.

1. **Qualified enquiry:** a non-spam enquiry from a real person or organisation with a relevant motion, video or animation need, genuine project intent and usable contact details.
2. **Sales-qualified enquiry:** a qualified enquiry with one or more stronger buying signals such as budget, timeline or decision authority.

**Measurement effect:** the north star counts qualified enquiries; the stronger tier is reported separately so genuine demand is not confused with sales readiness.

## OD-002 — 12-month north-star target

**Decision:** Defer the numerical target until one clean 28-day attribution baseline exists.

After the baseline is verified, the CEO proposes a realistic target range to Hailey. Ben approves the final range. No interim percentage or enquiry number may be invented.

## OD-003 — Financial authority

**Decision:** SGD 0 autonomous spend.

Every paid tool, placement, contractor, subscription, outreach commitment or other financial action requires Ben's approval through Hailey.

## OD-004 — Content cadence

**Decision:** Evidence-triggered quality cadence, with at most one publish candidate per week and no quota.

A candidate requires a clear KPI hypothesis and evidence-backed opportunity. Drafting does not authorise publication; each live publication remains separately approved and subject to Genesis website guardrails.

## OD-005 — Executive Telegram destination

**Decision:** Use one dedicated topic named **Genesis SEO — Executive**.

Only business-facing KPI changes, decisions, material risks and genuine owner blockers belong there. Technical logs and agent chatter are excluded. Delivery remains disabled until the exact topic/thread ID is created or identified and verified.

## OD-006 — Owner briefing cadence

**Decision:** Daily at 08:15 Asia/Singapore on material delta; otherwise silent.

Non-urgent developments may roll into a weekly summary. No "all clear" or no-change message is sent merely to satisfy a schedule.
