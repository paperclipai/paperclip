# OTLP field definitions

The field definitions in `eval-otlp-schema.ts` are derived from
[OpenTelemetry Protocol v1.9.0](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-proto/tree/v1.9.0).

Licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](./OTLP-LICENSE.txt).

The source comments and package declarations were removed to embed these field
definitions in the eval harness. The harness adds a combined response message.
Accounting behavior and limits are documented in [OPERATIONS.md](./OPERATIONS.md).
