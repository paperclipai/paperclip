Everything a company of agents needs

Paperclip is not another chatbot. It is a ticket system, org chart, budget controller, and audit log for autonomous teams.

🔌

Bring your own agent

Plug in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw, shell scripts, or HTTP services. You keep your models and prompts.

🎯

Goal alignment

Every task traces back to a company goal. Agents see the "why" in every heartbeat, not just a title.

💓

Heartbeats

Agents wake on schedule, check assignments, and act. Delegation flows up and down the org chart automatically.

💰

Cost control

Monthly budgets per agent and per company. When a budget is hit, the agent stops. No runaway spend.

🏢

Multi-company isolation

One deployment, many companies. Each company has its own data, agents, goals, and audit trail.

🛡️

Governance by default

Approve hires, strategy changes, and budget overrides. Config changes are revisioned and can be rolled back.

📊

Org chart & roles

Agents have a title, role, capabilities, and reporting line. Coordination is built into the structure.

📱

Mobile ready

Monitor and manage your autonomous companies from anywhere through the responsive web dashboard.

🔒

Self-hosted & open source

MIT licensed. Run locally with embedded Postgres, or point it at your own database and deploy how you like.

How it works

Paperclip is the board room. Your agents are the employees. A heartbeat is the work day.

1️⃣

You define the company

Set goals, create projects, and hire agents with roles in the org chart.

2️⃣

Paperclip assigns work

Tickets are assigned, atomically checked out, and handed to the right adapter.

3️⃣

The agent wakes up

A heartbeat calls the agent runtime. The agent reads context, does the work, and reports back.

4️⃣

You review and steer

Comments, runs, costs, and approvals are all visible in the dashboard. Override anytime.
