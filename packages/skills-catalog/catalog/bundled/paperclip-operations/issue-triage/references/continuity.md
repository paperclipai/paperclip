# Continuity during triage

Use the current task scope and the organization's existing release policy. A scoped wake takes precedence over an inbox pass. This skill grants no access, execution authority, budget, or change to the reporting structure. It does not schedule recurring work.

## Verify the wait

Check the actual interaction, approval, review, or dependency: recipient, state, relevance, and continuation. A historical or dismissed question does not block independent work. Apply an authenticated answer within the responder's authority and its conditions; preserve its source and never invent a response.

For a necessary human response, use a saved pending interaction and the supported waiting state while preserving the assignee. A technical consultation addresses the exact agent through `addresseeAgentId`, omits `addresseeUserId` and `resolverPolicy`, and requests `continuationPolicy: "wake_assignee"` when the answer must resume coordination. A consultation does not assign execution. Check the saved interaction set after related requests: supersession can invalidate an earlier pending path. Do not invent a confirmation to evade deduplication or recreate a dismissed question.

Keep work that awaits an explicit release or capacity in backlog. A premature admission does not grant release. Do not use a nudge, checkout, reassignment, or status change to bypass that wait. Do not represent capacity as a task dependency or pause another authorized task without authority.

Use `blockedByIssueIds` only for work that actually prevents the remaining delivery; `parentId` records origin. A cancelled dependency does not satisfy the blocker. Where native readiness reports `isDependencyReady=false`, `isUnresolved=true`, or `isPendingFinalize=true`, a `done` label alone does not release execution. Resolve coverage and finalization through the supported workflow. Continue independent authorized work. A completed child does not satisfy its parent's acceptance criteria by itself.

## Preserve ownership and the return path

Route environment, infrastructure, workspace, and admission failures to the designated technical owner under the organization's current policy. Record the concrete failure and available capabilities before escalation. Do not turn technical diagnosis into a product decision. Business rules, product intent, commercial priority, and scope changes use the authorized product decision flow. Use the current authenticated recipient; do not copy identities or organizational roles from another company.

Reuse an existing assigned task before creating a handoff. Execution requires an exact assignee, origin link, project or explicit absence of one, objective, original owner, impact, attempts, evidence, expected delivery, and acceptance criteria. Verify the saved assignment and initial plan. The source owner retains responsibility for the source delivery. Resolve work already assigned to you in that task. A mention or comment alone neither assigns execution nor proves a wake.

Before leaving, verify a supported continuation: assigned and released work, assigned backlog with a recorded coordination route and the next release event and responsible actor, a necessary pending interaction with continuation, or a real dependency path. Distinguish a persisted event, admission, receipt, and application. Record the returned source and the concrete change before declaring a handoff resolved. A successful submission does not prove incident recovery.

## Evidence and stopping conditions

Age and global agent status are investigation signals, not proof of this task's activity or failure. State when timing or cadence was not measured. Do not repeat a blocked update or escalate on elapsed heartbeats without a material new fact.

For skill integration, distinguish desired configuration, library version, runtime loading, reading, use, and application. Verify the effective consumer version and file contents when available. Null fields in one projection do not prove global absence of loading.

Keep permission denials and checkout conflicts authoritative. Do not retry the same denied action, change identity, or remove a control to bypass it. For a document revision conflict, read the current revision and reconcile before a bounded retry.

Record UTC, task/project/owner, source/revision, observation versus hypothesis, impact, attempt/result, limits, classification, action, continuation receipt, and next responsible actor. Exclude secrets. A heartbeat ends; claim continuous monitoring only when an authorized persistent mechanism exists.
